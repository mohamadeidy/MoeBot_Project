# Group 11 Visual Storage Policy — DRAFT

Group 11 must not create a screenshot for every setup/state/outcome row.

The official population artifact is a compact **visual recipe index** containing deterministic source IDs, chart window, timeframe, overlays, annotations, renderer version, and expected evidence links. PNG/JPEG artifacts are generated only for deterministic audit samples or on-demand investigation.

Large rendered audit batches are temporary/reconstructable. They are deleted after audit receipts/hashes are retained unless explicitly selected as canonical examples. This prevents visual data from becoming the dominant storage cost.
