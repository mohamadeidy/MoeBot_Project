# Group 14 Final Holdout Policy — DRAFT

Group 14 is the first stage allowed to make the final robustness decision on the frozen Group 13 policy.

2023 and 2024 have already participated in upstream engineering/validation and therefore cannot be treated as the sole final untouched holdout for Group 13 policy selection. Before Group 14 real execution, a holdout plan must be frozen that identifies data not used to design or tune the policy. Prefer additional years, symbols, and/or broker feeds when available.

If the final holdout fails, the result remains part of the evidence record. Any policy redesign creates a new policy version and requires a new untouched evaluation protocol; the failed holdout is not relabeled as training data and reused as if untouched.
